# Content Flow

## Applet 1 – Light and Its Properties

### Learning Goal

Students discover that:

* We need light to see objects.
* Some objects produce their own light.
* Light travels in a straight line.
* Light passes through transparent materials.
* Opaque objects block light and form shadows.
* Materials can be transparent, translucent or opaque.
